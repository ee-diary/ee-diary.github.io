Raspberry Pi Pico 2 Interactive Pinout Explorer User Guide 

Welcome to the ultimate Interactive Raspberry Pi Pico 2 Board Explorer, a web-based companion designed for embedded developers, students, and hardware hobbyists working with the powerful RP2350 microcontroller. Simply hover over or click any physical pin on the high-definition PCB graphic to instantly reveal its dedicated voltage levels, alternate pin multiplexing, and hardware properties. Use the Highlight by Function panel to isolate specific peripheral groups like hardware SPI, I2C, PWM, or Programmable I/O (PIO) blocks at a glance or map out the board's internal layout using the Highlight by Component tool. Complete with direct access to official schematics, PCB footprints, and 3D CAD models, this interactive tool eliminates the need to scour massive documentation, streamlining your firmware development and circuit prototyping workflow.
