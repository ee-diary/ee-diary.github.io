The ACS758 series from Allegro MicroSystems is a Hall-effect linear current sensor designed for both AC and DC current measurement across industrial, automotive, and consumer applications.

## **Key Specifications & Features**

• Status: Not recommended for new designs; consider ACS772 or ACS773 as alternatives.  

• Current Ranges: Offered in bidirectional (±50A, ±100A, ±150A, ±200A) and unidirectional versions.  

• Sensitivity:  

 • ±50A (Bidirectional): 40 mV/A  

 • ±100A (Bidirectional): 20 mV/A  

 • ±150A (Bidirectional): 13.3 mV/A  

 • ±200A (Bidirectional): 10 mV/A  

• Output Voltage: Ratiometric analog output; bidirectional models provide \(V_{CC}/2\) (2.5V at 5V supply) at zero current.  

• Supply Voltage: 3.0V to 5.5V DC.  

• Internal Conductor Resistance: Typically, 100 μΩ for minimal power loss.  

• Galvanic Isolation: Withstands 4800 \(V_{RMS}\) isolation voltage.  

• Bandwidth & Response Time: 120 kHz typical bandwidth, 3 µs rise time.  

## Typical Applications

• Motor control and inverter feedback  

• Solar and photovoltaic monitoring  

• Electric vehicle (EV) battery management systems  

• Overcurrent and overload protection  

• Uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) and DC-DC converters
